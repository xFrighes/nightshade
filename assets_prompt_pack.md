# Asset Prompt Pack: Dark Low-Fantasy RPG

This pack contains prompts for generating assets that match the mood and style of the provided reference image.

## General Art Style
- **Style**: High-quality 16-bit or 32-bit pixel art.
- **Atmosphere**: Dark low-fantasy, moody, medieval urban night.
- **Palette**: Deep slate blues, cool purples, warm lantern oranges, charcoal greys.
- **Technique**: Clean lines, readable silhouettes, dithered shading for shadows.

## 1. Environment: Starting Location
- **Prompt**: Pixel art, 2.5D belt-scroll side-view, dark medieval city street at night. Stone cobblestone ground, timber-framed buildings with textured stone walls. Moody hanging lanterns with a warm orange glow. Subtle blue fog in the distance. Background shows rooftops against a dim moonlit sky. High contrast between cool shadows and warm lights. --ar 16:9

## 2. Character: Player (Nightshade Initiate)
- **Prompt**: Pixel art character sprite, hooded rogue, dark leather armor, weathered grey cloak. Neutral pose, side-view. 32x64 pixels. Clear silhouette. Dark fantasy aesthetic. --v 6.0

## 3. NPCs
- **The Guide (Old Guard)**: Pixel art character sprite, grizzled veteran, heavy chainmail under a dark blue surcoat, leaning on a spear. Lantern hanging from the belt. --v 6.0
- **The Merchant (Shadow Broker)**: Pixel art character sprite, mysterious figure in a heavy cloak with many pouches, partially obscured face, sitting on a wooden crate. --v 6.0
- **The Mysterious Contact**: Pixel art character sprite, slender figure in an ornate dark purple robe, holding a small glowing rune stone. --v 6.0

## 4. UI Elements
- **Item Icons**: Pixel art 16x16 or 32x32 icons: rusted key, leather pouch, glowing rune, iron dagger, stale bread.
- **Dialog Box**: Pixel art ornate stone-textured frame for UI dialog, dark translucent background.

---
*Note: Use these prompts in Midjourney, DALL-E 3, or Stable Diffusion (A1111/Forge) to generate the final assets.*
